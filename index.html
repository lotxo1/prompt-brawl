Prompt Brawl

Describe a fighter in one line. Watch it brawl.

The AI designs your fighter from that line (look, moves, quirks and weaknesses) and throws it into a commentated battle. Clever descriptions win.

Free to play with your Pollinations account: new accounts get free Pollen, and a fight costs about 0.01 Pollen. You pay with your own Pollen; nobody else's key runs dry.

  1. Write one line“A grandma with a cast-iron frying pan who never loses her cool.”
  2. Meet your fighterA trading card with a portrait, stats, three moves, a quirk and a weakness.
  3. Brawl and climbBeat six challengers, face the daily boss and share your best fights.

Who's stepping into the arena?

Specific beats generic: give it a job, a habit, a fear or a weird item.
